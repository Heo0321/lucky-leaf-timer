1. Overview

Developer Heonix ("Developer") values the privacy of users of "Lucky Leaf Timer" (the "App") and complies with applicable laws and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as name or phone number. However, users who opt-in to the backup feature may provide their email address.

  • Focus Records: User's focus and break time sessions
  • Age Group: Optional demographic information (10s/20s/30s/40s+) for statistics comparison
  • Email Address: Collected only when users enable backup/restore functionality
  • Advertising ID: Generated by Google AdMob for ad serving

3. Data Storage and Purpose

  • Local Storage: Focus records are stored locally on device (SharedPreferences/Room)
  • Server Storage: Data is backed up to Firebase Firestore for cross-device restoration under an anonymous UID or user email
  • Analytics: Age-group statistics are aggregated for comparative analysis without identifying individual users

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Google services, each governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Auth, Firestore): User authentication and data backup
  • Google AdMob: Ad serving and analytics
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchase (ad removal) processing
  • Google Play In-App Review: In-app rating request
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Security

All data transmitted to servers is encrypted and securely managed. When a user deletes their account, all associated server data is permanently deleted immediately.

6. Your Rights

  • You can delete your account and records at any time via the app settings
  • You can disable ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device settings

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
